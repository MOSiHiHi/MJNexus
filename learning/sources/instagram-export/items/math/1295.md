## خلاصهٔ استخراجی (از کپشن)
The Right-Angle Walk That Finds Real Roots In 1867 Eduard Lill found a way to read a polynomial's real roots straight off a drawing. You turn its coefficients into a right-angled path, then fire one line that reflects corner to corner until it lands on the end.
